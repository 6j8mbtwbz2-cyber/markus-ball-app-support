Privacy Policy for GeniusHDR

for GeniusHDR GainMap Studio and its support website

Last updated: September 8, 2026

1. Data processed by the app

GeniusHDR GainMap Studio processes image files that you open yourself or pass to the app from another application. This includes image data, embedded color profiles, and technically required metadata such as image dimensions, file format, and, where available, capture date.

The app does not create a user account or collect personal data for me. It does not use proprietary analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Images, image content, and usage profiles are not transmitted to me.

The app stores settings on your Mac, including output format, quality, appearance, filename rules, and workflow options. It may also store security-scoped bookmarks created by macOS for selected input and output folders, together with the associated path information. This information remains in the app's local settings and is used solely to restore the selected functions the next time the app starts.

2. Local image processing

Reading, checking, displaying, and converting images, as well as generating and embedding the gain map, take place locally on your Mac. GeniusHDR uses macOS system technologies and image codecs bundled locally with the app. The app does not upload your images to a server or online service for HDR processing.

GeniusHDR accesses only files and folders that you select, pass to the app, or authorize through macOS security mechanisms. Saved folder permissions may become invalid if you select another folder, remove the relevant app settings, or change their management in macOS.

For some processing steps, the app may briefly create files in macOS's local temporary directory. These temporary files are removed after the respective operation. Exported files are stored only in the destination you choose. Local completion notifications may display a filename, output format, and destination folder; they are delivered by macOS and are not sent to me.

3. Support website and contact

The GeniusHDR support website is a static website delivered through GitHub Pages. The provider is GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. For users in the European Economic Area, GitHub also identifies GitHub B.V., Prins Bernhardplein 200, 1097 JB Amsterdam, the Netherlands.

When you visit the website, your browser connects to GitHub. GitHub processes technically necessary connection data, in particular the IP address, and may process further information such as request time, requested file, referrer, browser, operating system, and device details. GitHub states that visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes when a GitHub Pages site is visited.

The legal basis for providing the website is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is the secure, reliable, and economical provision of support information. GitHub may process data in the United States and other countries. Further information is available in the GitHub Privacy Statement. I do not control GitHub's specific retention periods.

The website's own code does not set cookies or use analytics, advertising, or tracking services. External content is not embedded automatically; an external provider is contacted only after you select the corresponding link.

If you contact me by email, I process your email address, the content of your message, and the information it contains in order to respond. Depending on the subject, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR. The data is deleted once the request has been conclusively handled and no statutory retention duty or legitimate reason requires further storage.

4. App Store and external services

When you obtain or update the app through the Mac App Store, Apple processes data under its own responsibility, including information concerning the Apple Account, download, purchase processing, updates, device, and, where applicable, diagnostics. I do not receive payment details and cannot access your Apple Account password. See Apple's App Store privacy information and Privacy Policy.

The image codecs bundled with GeniusHDR operate locally and do not themselves connect to Google, Adobe, or other providers. Technical namespaces used in image metadata are format identifiers only and are not internet requests.

Links to support, privacy information, or other websites open in your web browser only after you select them. The privacy terms of the respective website provider also apply from that point onward.

5. Your rights

Subject to the legal requirements, you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection. You may withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The competent authority in Hesse is the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

Because image processing and app settings remain locally on your Mac and are not transmitted to me, I do not have access to this data. You manage it directly on your device.
