Privacy Policy for FotoStack Importer

for the FotoStack Importer macOS app and its support website

Last updated: September 8, 2026

1. Data processed by the app

FotoStack Importer processes files from source and destination folders you select. It reads filenames, file size and timestamps, and technically required image metadata such as capture date, camera model, exposure values, and, where available, focus-bracketing information. This data is used for preview, focus- and HDR-series detection, duplicate checking, and filing.

The app does not create a user account, use proprietary analytics, advertising, or tracking services, or transmit images, metadata, or usage profiles to me. Its privacy manifest declares no collected data types and no tracking.

Import, detection, folder, sidecar, and appearance settings are stored locally. For persistently selected photo and destination folders, macOS stores protected security-scoped bookmarks together with the corresponding path information.

2. Local image and file processing

Reading, comparing, grouping, and copying images and sidecar files take place locally on your Mac. The app does not upload files to an online service. It accesses only folders you select or authorize through macOS.

During import, the app creates copies in the selected destination and may create subfolders, sidecar backups, and series folders according to your settings. Cancel and undo can remove only destination copies created by the app during the relevant import; source files remain unchanged. If sidecar updating is enabled, an existing destination sidecar may be replaced and a local backup can optionally be created first.

3. Support website and contact

The support website is delivered statically through GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technically necessary connection data, in particular the IP address, and may process request time, requested file, browser, operating system, and device details. GitHub states that IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes when GitHub Pages sites are visited.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is secure and reliable support delivery. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. The website's own code does not set cookies or use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

If you contact me by email, I process the sender address, message, and information it contains to handle your request under Article 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR. The data is deleted after completion unless statutory retention duties or legitimate reasons require otherwise.

4. App Store and external services

When you obtain or update the app through the Mac App Store, Apple processes data under its own responsibility, including information concerning the Apple Account, download, purchase processing, updates, device, and, where applicable, diagnostics. I do not receive payment details and cannot access your Apple Account password. See Apple's App Store privacy information.

Support and legal links open in your browser only after you select them. The privacy terms of the respective provider also apply from that point onward.

5. Your rights

Subject to legal requirements, you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, and objection. You may withdraw consent at any time for the future. You may also complain to the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information or another competent authority.

I have no access to images, metadata, folder permissions, and app settings processed locally; you manage them directly on your Mac.
