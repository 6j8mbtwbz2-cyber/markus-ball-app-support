2. Music library and local storage

The app processes music from selected media servers, authorized folders and, after system permission on iPhone and iPad, the locally available music library. This includes audio and metadata such as title, artist, album, genre, year, track number, duration and artwork, plus technically required file and stream addresses, source and device identifiers, device names, network addresses and security-scoped folder bookmarks.

Library indexes, artwork, playlists, favorites, recent sources, playback and appearance settings are stored on your device. Local SHA-256 file fingerprints may be calculated to recognize music files. File paths and modification information used for this comparison remain in the local cache.

“Recently played” stores track identifiers and playback timestamps locally. When pruned, the history is limited to at most 500 entries from the last 90 days. You can clear it using “Clear history”; this history is not included in collection sync.

3. Music sources, artwork and output devices

Device discovery sends and receives local network requests, including SSDP/UPnP and Bonjour. Discovered devices provide names, models, manufacturers, identifiers, control endpoints and icon addresses. Device icons are loaded from these addresses; which devices and brands appear depends on your network.

When browsing or playing music, the app communicates with selected sources and outputs, such as a FRITZ!Box media server or Yamaha receiver. Depending on the function, this exchanges library requests, track and artwork addresses, metadata, audio, playback positions and commands for playback, volume or mute. Recipients can see your device’s connection address. Direct network players may retrieve music from the source themselves.

Artwork comes from music files or addresses supplied by the music source. No separate external artwork search service is integrated. Sources pointing to internet addresses can cause requests to those providers. Local UPnP/HTTP connections are not necessarily encrypted; the app does not replace a trusted local network.

To make supported local MP3 files available to a network player, the app creates temporary copies and a local HTTP endpoint with random media addresses. The audio can be retrieved at these addresses from the reachable network. The temporary files are removed when this serving session ends. AirPlay and Bluetooth use the respective Apple system functions.

4. Automatic iCloud Drive collection sync

Collection sync starts automatically when iCloud Drive is available to the app. It transfers playlist names and track assignments, track and album favorites, saved favorite colors and music descriptions needed to recognize entries. These may include titles, albums, artists, genres, years, durations, file fingerprints and origin information such as source names, server identifiers, server host addresses, device names and device identifiers. Revisions and deletion markers keep multiple devices consistent.

Collection sync does not transfer music files, artwork files, local folder paths or folder permissions. Current network discovery, selected output, offline copies and active audio and appearance settings remain device-specific. If you choose an iCloud or other cloud folder for music or downloads yourself, that provider may sync these files independently of collection sync.

Data resides in your Apple Account’s iCloud Drive area. Without available iCloud Drive the app uses its local state. Manage iCloud Drive access in your Apple Account system settings. Turning it off does not automatically delete existing cloud copies; manage these separately in iCloud Drive.

5. Device remote control and keychain

“Allow device control” is enabled by default on first launch. With network access available, the app advertises a Bonjour service containing a random app-device identifier and device name. The name can come from your system device name and is visible to devices on the reachable local network.

Remote-control connections between LocalCast devices use TLS with a pairing key. To associate your own Apple devices automatically, the app publishes its pairing key in the synchronizable iCloud Keychain when available. Manual pairing is also possible. Local keys are stored in the system keychain. I do not receive these keys.

Connected devices can exchange playback state, track metadata and network artwork addresses, device and output names, position, duration, queue, timer state and technical error messages, and send commands. Stream addresses and local artwork file addresses are removed before this exchange; track identifiers can still contain source-related information. Remote control itself does not transfer audio files. Devices on the same Apple Account may connect automatically when the matching key is available.

Disable device control under “Devices” to end remote-control connections, stop Bonjour advertising and request removal of this device’s synchronized remote-control key from iCloud Keychain. Under “Advanced” you can change the device name or renew the code to replace the previous access key. Normal music-source and receiver control are separate functions.

6. Permissions and Apple system features

The app uses system permissions for the local network, selected files and, where applicable, the music library. Outputs such as Bluetooth and AirPlay are selected through the operating system. You can change permissions in your device’s privacy and app settings, which may restrict the related feature. The app does not request or offer its own Apple Music subscription; device-library import uses accessible local, unprotected tracks.

Title, artist, album, artwork and playback state may be passed to the system’s Now Playing interface and appear on the lock screen, Control Center or connected outputs. The sleep timer may appear as a locally updated Live Activity on iPhone, with no app push server. App Shortcuts expose selected music and device actions through Apple’s system features. Siri use is additionally governed by your Siri settings.

7. Offline copies and chosen storage locations

Offline downloads are retrieved from your selected music source and saved to your chosen destination folder. The app stores download status, file associations and the required folder bookmark. You can delete offline copies in the app. Files in user-selected folders, external drives or cloud folders may remain independently of uninstalling the app.

8. Diagnostic logs and support

The app writes a bounded local audio diagnostic log for troubleshooting. It may contain timestamps, app and operating system versions, audio-output and device names, technical playback states, timings, format information, HTTP status codes and errors. When it exceeds approximately 256 KiB, the file is reduced to a more recent portion; it has no fixed time-based retention period. Audio is not recorded and logs are not sent automatically.

Creating an audio diagnostic report produces an exportable report. Data is shared only when you choose “Share log” and select a recipient or destination. Review it for personal information in device names or errors before sharing. Apple’s separate system diagnostics depend on your Apple privacy settings.

If you send me an email, report or screenshot, I process your sender address, message, attachments and the device and error information you provide to handle your inquiry. Your selected service handles sending; my email provider handles receiving. Further disclosure occurs only as needed to respond, required by law or initiated by you.

9. Recipients and external services

Recipients depend on the features used: your media servers and output devices, connected LocalCast devices, Apple for iCloud Drive, iCloud Keychain and other system services, any selected file or cloud providers, and the email services involved and me when you contact support.

Apple processes Apple Account, App Store, download, update and optionally shared diagnostic data under its own terms. iCloud and other online services may process data outside the European Economic Area. The respective provider’s information and transfer arrangements apply; storage exclusively within the EU is not promised. See the Apple Privacy Policy, iCloud Keychain & Privacy and App Store & Privacy.

LocalCast Audio does not integrate its own advertising, tracking or usage-analytics SDKs and does not match your music to advertising profiles.

10. Visiting the support website

The support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technically required connection and request data, particularly your IP address, and may process request time, requested file, browser and device information. GitHub explicitly states that visitor IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes on GitHub Pages.

Our own page code does not set cookies or use analytics or advertising services. Simply reading the bundled offline help does not contact the support website. See the additional website privacy information and GitHub Privacy Statement, including its international processing and retention information. GitHub, rather than the app, determines how long its logs are retained.

11. Purposes and legal bases

Where I process personal data as controller, providing requested app features and contractual or pre-contractual assistance is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Secure hosting of support information, troubleshooting and other inquiries are based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interests are operational security and functioning support. Legally required retention is based on Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

Where separate data-protection consent is required and obtained, Article 6(1)(a) GDPR applies; consent may be withdrawn for the future. Operating-system permission is a technical access authorization and is not treated here as blanket consent to all processing. Independent Apple, device and cloud providers additionally apply their own legal bases.

Support information is voluntary. Without a contact address and sufficient error details I may be unable to handle the inquiry. Basic app use does not require contacting support. There is no automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects or associated profiling.

12. Retention and deletion

Local libraries, settings and collections remain stored while retained for your use. Manage playlists, favorites, history and offline copies using their respective functions. Removing a source or favorite does not necessarily delete all cached data or saved descriptions. A complete cleanup may also require managing local app data, selected download folders, iCloud Drive data, keychain entries and device backups.

Cross-device sync can retain technical deletion markers and recognition descriptions. Uninstalling the app or disabling iCloud therefore does not delete every cloud, keychain or backup copy. I have no direct access to these data; manage them on your devices and with the providers involved.

Support messages and attachments are deleted once the inquiry has been conclusively handled and no continuing purpose, legal retention duty or legitimate reason, such as resolving claims, requires retention. Documents subject to legal retention duties are retained for the prescribed period.
