Plan your baking day in five steps

  1. Create a recipe

    Open “My Breads” and create a new bread recipe. Enter the number of loaves and the desired finished weight per loaf.

  2. Choose flour and ingredients

    Set flour shares to a total of 100%. Add other ingredients and review the suggested sourdough process.

  3. Review and save

    Check water, salt and the recipe description. Save the recipe to find it in “My Breads”.

  4. Plan your baking day

    Choose the planning action on the recipe card. Start as soon as possible or choose a desired finish time, and check your rest window.

  5. Start the schedule

    Review the schedule and reminders. Start your baking day and complete each working step as you go.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate several loaves at once?

Set the loaf count and finished weight per loaf separately in the first step. Ingredients are scaled to the entire batch. The baking profile still depends on the weight of a single loaf.

Read more in the manual

Why does the water amount change with the flour blend?

Each flour has its own water absorption factor. The app calculates each flour separately and accounts for water in scalds and soakers. Hydration adjustment lets you adapt the water amount to your flour batch and experience.

Read more in the manual

Which sourdough processes are calculated automatically?

BrotBackstubb automatically supports rye, wheat and spelt sourdough. Other flours may be used in the main dough, but do not receive automatic sourdough values derived from these three. Blends containing rye use a rye sourdough process.

Read more in the manual

Why can’t I start with my desired finish time?

The plan accounts for the current time and your rest window. If the baking day would already have needed to start, a warning appears. Use the suggested “as soon as possible” option to plan again from now.

Read more in the manual

Why is the total remaining time longer than the next working step?

Total remaining time includes all remaining working, fermentation, resting, proofing and baking time until the bread is finished. It therefore exceeds the duration of the next working step.

Read more in the manual

How do I collect ingredients from several recipes?

Add recipes to the shopping list using their card menus. Matching ingredients are combined. Check stock and shopping quantities in the list, then save them to your selected Reminders list if needed.

Read more in the manual

Can I export a recipe as a PDF or for Mela?

Yes. Open the recipe card’s action menu to view or save a PDF, or export a Mela recipe file (.melarecipe).

Read more in the manual

How do I back up recipes or recover a deleted recipe?

Save a recipe version to keep a restorable state. Deleted recipes remain in the trash for 30 days. Export a data backup to save recipes and the ingredient database together, and import it later to restore the backup.

Read more in the manual
