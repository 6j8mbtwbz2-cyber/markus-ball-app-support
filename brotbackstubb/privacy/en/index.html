Privacy Policy for BrotBackstubb

for BrotBackstubb on Mac and iPad and its support website

Last updated: October 15, 2026

1. Scope and local data

This policy covers BrotBackstubb for macOS and iPadOS and its support website. The app processes the recipes, ingredients, quantities, notes, descriptions, shopping lists and baking schedules you enter. Recipe data, catalog changes, revisions, trash, active baking days and preferences are stored in the local application data area of each device.

BrotBackstubb requires no user account and operates no proprietary server for your recipes. It includes no proprietary analytics, advertising or tracking services and does not transmit your recipes or schedules to me. Automatic synchronization of recipe libraries between Mac and iPad is not built in. Device or system backups you enable may include app data, subject to the settings and terms of the backup service.

2. Shopping lists and Apple Reminders

When you transfer a shopping list to Apple Reminders, the app requests full access to reminders. With your permission, it reads available reminder lists and entries in the selected destination list. It can create its own list, save ingredients and quantities as reminders, update them, and remove its own incomplete entries that are no longer needed. Entries managed by the app are identified using a marker.

The destination list may belong to a local account, iCloud or another account configured in Reminders. If that account synchronizes data, its provider processes these entries and may transfer them to other devices according to your account settings. I do not receive access to them. The app does not create calendar events.

You can leave this transfer unused or revoke Reminders access in System Settings or Settings under Privacy & Security. Local recipe management remains available. Revoking access does not automatically delete previously transferred entries or lists; you can manage and delete these in Apple Reminders.

3. Notifications and alarms

For upcoming baking steps, the app passes step names and times to the local notification system. On supported iPads, the selected continuous alarm mode can also use Apple AlarmKit, which requires a separate system alarm permission. Scheduled notifications or alarms are updated or cancelled as you operate the baking day. These features do not use push messages from a proprietary server.

You can manage or disable notifications, previews and alarm permissions in operating system settings. Depending on your settings, step names and times may be visible while the device is locked. Revoking permission can limit the relevant notification or alarm feature.

4. Widgets and Live Activities

The app and its widget extension share a local data area on the same device. Display data includes the recipe name, current or next step, target time, and start and end of the baking schedule. On Mac this supports desktop and Notification Center widgets. On iPad it supports Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets; Live Activities also pass corresponding status data to the operating system.

Widgets and Live Activities can display recipe and step names and times outside the app window. On iPad, this information may be visible to others on the Lock Screen. You can remove widgets and manage available display or Live Activity options in system settings. When you end a baking day normally, the app removes its widget status and ends the Live Activity. A previous display may remain until the system refreshes it. These features do not transmit the data to me.

5. Recipe descriptions with Apple Intelligence

If you request an automatic recipe description on a supported device, the app processes selected recipe and ingredient information using Apple’s local language model through the Foundation Models framework. The generated description is stored locally as part of your recipe if you keep it. For this feature, BrotBackstubb uses no external AI service and does not transmit recipe information to a server operated by me.

6. Import, export and deletion

Calculations, imports and exports take place locally. Backup files and PDF, Markdown or Mela exports are stored at the location you select or passed on through the system dialog you choose. Temporary local files may be created for previews and sharing. If you select iCloud Drive, another cloud storage provider, email or another app, that service processes the selected content according to your settings.

Recipes can be restored from the trash or permanently deleted there; revisions, backups and exports may contain additional copies that you need to manage separately. App data is retained on your device to provide the features you use. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily remove all local Mac data, system backups, exports or reminders already transferred. I cannot access or remotely delete this data.

7. Support website and contact

The support website is delivered statically through GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technically necessary connection data, in particular the IP address, and may process request time, requested file, browser, operating system, and device details. GitHub states that IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes when GitHub Pages sites are visited.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is secure and reliable support delivery. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. The website's own code does not set cookies or use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

If you contact me by email, I process the sender address, message, and information it contains to handle your request under Article 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR. The data is deleted after completion unless statutory retention duties or legitimate reasons require otherwise.

8. App Store and external services

When you obtain or update the app through the App Store or Mac App Store, Apple processes data under its own responsibility, including information concerning the Apple Account, download, purchase processing, updates, device, and, where applicable, diagnostics. I do not receive payment details and cannot access your Apple Account password. See Apple's App Store privacy information.

Support and legal links open in your browser only after you select them. The privacy terms of the respective provider also apply from that point onward.

9. Your rights

Subject to legal requirements, you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, and objection. You may withdraw consent at any time for the future. You may also complain to the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information or another competent authority.

I have no access to locally stored recipes, schedules, settings, and notifications; you manage them directly on your device.
