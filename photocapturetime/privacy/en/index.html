Privacy Policy for PhotoCaptureTime

for the PhotoCaptureTime macOS app and its support website

Last updated: September 8, 2026

1. Data processed by the app

PhotoCaptureTime processes only files that you select yourself. The app reads filenames and technically required image metadata, in particular capture time, file time, and, where available, camera model. This information is used to check time differences, propose new names, and group files according to your instructions.

The app does not create a user account or collect personal data for me. It does not use proprietary analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Files, metadata, and usage profiles are not transmitted to me.

Appearance options, filename components, and camera abbreviations you enter are stored locally in the app settings on your Mac.

2. Local file processing

Analysis, preview, and renaming take place locally on your Mac. PhotoCaptureTime does not upload images or metadata to a server. The app accesses only files and folders you authorize through the macOS file picker.

When you confirm renaming, the selected files are renamed at their existing location. Local intermediate names may be used briefly to avoid name collisions; if an error occurs, the app attempts to restore the original state. The app does not create a cloud copy or transmit filenames to me.

3. Support website and contact

The support website is a static website delivered through GitHub Pages. When it is visited, GitHub processes technically necessary connection data, in particular the IP address, and may process request time, requested file, browser, operating system, and device details. GitHub states that IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes when GitHub Pages sites are visited.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is the secure and reliable provision of support information. Further details are available in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The website's own code does not set cookies or use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

If you contact me by email, I process the sender address, message, and information it contains to handle your request under Article 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR. The data is deleted after completion unless statutory retention duties or legitimate reasons require otherwise.

4. App Store and external services

When you obtain or update the app through the Mac App Store, Apple processes data under its own responsibility, including information concerning the Apple Account, download, purchase processing, updates, device, and, where applicable, diagnostics. I do not receive payment details and cannot access your Apple Account password. See Apple's App Store privacy information.

Support and legal links open in your browser only after you select them. The privacy terms of the respective provider also apply from that point onward.

5. Your rights

Subject to legal requirements, you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, and objection. You may withdraw consent at any time for the future. You may also complain to a supervisory authority, in particular the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

I have no access to files and app settings processed locally; you manage them directly on your Mac.
